About Our Organization

More than a plant shop.
We are a sanctuary.

Flora & Pot began as a tiny greenhouse experiment and blossomed into a full-scale botanical organization. We believe that integrating greenery into indoor environments improves mental health, purifies the air, and brings unparalleled tranquility to our fast-paced lives.

Our dedicated team of horticulturists, designers, and plant lovers work tirelessly to source the healthiest, most resilient plants from ethical nurseries around the world, ensuring they are ready to thrive in your unique home environment.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

We are guided by a simple philosophy: bringing the profound beauty and calming energy of the natural world directly into your daily life.

Our Vision

To cultivate a world where every indoor space is enriched by the presence of living plants. We envision a future where urban environments seamlessly blend with nature, fostering healthier, happier, and more sustainable communities globally.

Our Mission

To provide expertly nurtured, ethically sourced greenery alongside accessible education. We strive to empower plant parents of all experience levels with the knowledge and high-quality botanicals needed to make their indoor gardens thrive.

Our Journey

How We Grew

From a humble backyard greenhouse to a beloved community staple, our roots have always been grounded in a genuine love for botany and design.
2018

The Seed is Planted

Flora & Pot started as a small passion project in a local garage. We began by propagating rare clippings and selling them at weekend farmer's markets to local enthusiasts.

2021

Our First Flagship

Due to overwhelming community support, we opened our first physical retail space. We partnered with local ceramists to offer custom-crafted pots alongside our greenery.

2024

A National Reach

We expanded our operations to include a state-of-the-art shipping facility, allowing us to safely deliver healthy, beautiful plants to homes all across the country.
