Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with Flora & Pot.

Shipping & Orders

How do you safely ship live plants?

We use custom-engineered, eco-friendly corrugated packaging that completely secures the nursery pot and protects the foliage from being crushed. For winter shipments, we automatically include complimentary heat packs to prevent freezing during transit.

What is your 30-Day Guarantee?

We guarantee that your plant will arrive in happy, healthy condition. If your plant dies or arrives severely damaged within the first 30 days of receipt, simply email us a photo and your order number. We will issue a replacement or a full refund, no questions asked.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, due to strict agricultural and customs regulations regarding live soil and plants, we only ship within the contiguous United States. We hope to expand to Canada and Mexico in the near future.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is processed and leaves our greenhouse, you will receive an email containing a tracking number and a link to our carrier's website. You can also view live tracking updates via the "Track Order" button in your user dashboard.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and offer split-payment options through Afterpay for larger orders and subscription plans.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Because we process live plant orders quickly to minimize transit time, we can only accept cancellations or modifications within 2 hours of placing the order. Please contact our support team immediately if you need assistance.

Plant Care & Services

Do you offer repotting services for plants not bought here?

Yes! Our in-store repotting and rehabilitation clinic is open to all plants, regardless of where they were purchased. Pricing is based on the diameter of the pot and the type of organic soil required for your specific plant species.

Are your plants safe for cats and dogs?

Not all of them, but we make it easy to find the ones that are. We have a dedicated "Pet-Friendly" category in our shop. All plants in this section are verified non-toxic to cats and dogs according to ASPCA guidelines.

How do I cancel or modify my subscription plan?

You can easily pause, modify, or cancel your maintenance subscription at any time by logging into the "Customer Login" portal. Navigating to the "Subscriptions" tab will give you full control over your billing cycle and service dates.

What should I do if my plant arrives with yellow leaves?

It is perfectly normal for a plant to lose a bottom leaf or two due to transit shock. Give it a few days to acclimate to your home's lighting before watering. If the yellowing spreads rapidly, please reach out to our support team with a photo!

Do you provide ongoing care advice after purchase?

Absolutely! Every order includes a detailed digital care card. Additionally, you are always welcome to email our botanists with pictures of your plant for free, lifetime troubleshooting and advice.

How often should I water my new indoor plant?

Watering frequency depends heavily on the plant species, your home's humidity, and light exposure. Always check the top 2 inches of soil with your finger before watering. If it feels dry, it's usually safe to water. Check your specific plant's care card for precise details.
